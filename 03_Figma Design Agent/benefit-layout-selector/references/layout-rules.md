# Layout Rules

This reference maps benefit-point count and information hierarchy to the bundled reference images in `../assets/aesthetic-reference/`.

## Quick Decision Table

| Benefit count | Recommended layouts |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `上下布局` for a strong visual statement; `左右布局-屏效` for a lightweight horizontal module |
| 2 | Equal priority: `左右布局` or `上下布局`; hierarchy: `主次`; limited height: `屏效` |
| 3 | One core plus two auxiliaries: `上下布局-主次`; equal priority: `左右布局` or `上下布局`; integrated scene: `包含布局` |
| 4 | Equal priority: `四宫格布局`; reading-heavy: `上下布局`; one core plus supporting points: `上下布局-主次` or `包含布局` |
| 5 | One core plus four/five supporting points: `上下布局-主次` or `左右布局-主次`; integrated package: `包含布局` |

## Layout Type Definitions

| Type | Meaning | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上下布局 | Benefits arranged vertically, read top to bottom | Higher information density, mobile-friendly reading |
| 左右布局 | Benefits arranged horizontally | Desktop modules, feature cards, parallel selling points |
| 主次布局 | One benefit is visually larger than the rest | One core selling point with supporting details |
| 包含布局 | A large container holds several smaller benefits | Integrated product scenes, benefit packages, module-level unity |
| 屏效布局 | A flat, compact, screen-like horizontal area | Limited height, product UI feeling, lightweight transitions |
| 四宫格布局 | Four benefits in a 2x2 grid | Four equal-priority items needing balance and order |

## 1 Benefit Point

### 模块-1个利益点-上下布局.png

- Structure: Title and subtitle at the top left; one wide content area below.
- Relationship: Single core benefit.
- Best for: One complete selling point, proposition, or product capability.
- Suggestion: Use the content area for a large image, product screenshot, core metric, or strong benefit statement.

### 模块-1个利益点-左右布局-屏效.png

- Structure: Title and subtitle on the left; one horizontal content area on the right.
- Relationship: Single benefit with text and visual separated.
- Best for: Limited page height, product screenshot, short selling point, status bar, or screen-like visual.
- Suggestion: Keep the right area simple.

## 2 Benefit Points

### 模块-2个利益点-上下布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; one large content area; one smaller content area below.
- Relationship: First benefit is primary, second is auxiliary.
- Best for: Core value followed by supporting value.

### 模块-2个利益点-上下布局.png

- Structure: Title area; two equal horizontal content areas stacked vertically.
- Relationship: Two benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Two parallel selling points, steps, or capability modules.

### 模块-2个利益点-左右布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; large left content area and smaller right content area.
- Relationship: Left is primary, right is auxiliary.
- Best for: One core selling point plus one support point in a horizontal module.

### 模块-2个利益点-左右布局-屏效.png

- Structure: Title area on the left; two small content areas on the right.
- Relationship: Two lightweight equal benefits.
- Best for: Product UI effects, short selling points, icon explanations, compact modules.

### 模块-2个利益点-左右布局.png

- Structure: Title area; two equal content areas side by side.
- Relationship: Two benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Two feature cards, two方案, or two parallel advantages.

## 3 Benefit Points

### 模块-3个利益点-上下布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; one large content area; two smaller content areas below.
- Relationship: One core benefit plus two auxiliary benefits.
- Best for: One strongest selling point supported by two details.
- Suggestion: Put the main visual or strongest conclusion in the large area; put short text, icons, or data in the two smaller areas.

### 模块-3个利益点-上下布局.png

- Structure: Title area; three equal content areas stacked vertically.
- Relationship: Three benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Three features, three steps, or three categories of benefits.

### 模块-3个利益点-包含布局.png

- Structure: Title area; one large container with two smaller embedded areas.
- Relationship: Three benefits belong to one integrated scene, usually one main plus two supporting.
- Best for: Product highlight pages, activity benefit modules, and cohesive visual packages.

### 模块-3个利益点-左右布局-主次-屏效.png

- Structure: Title area on the left; one larger vertical main content area; two smaller areas on the far right.
- Relationship: One core benefit plus two auxiliary benefits in a horizontal screen-effect composition.
- Best for: A primary product interface or effect with two supporting abilities.

### 模块-3个利益点-左右布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; large left content area; two smaller right content areas.
- Relationship: One main benefit and two secondary benefits.
- Best for: Desktop product capability aggregation.

### 模块-3个利益点-左右布局-屏效.png

- Structure: Title area on the left; three small content areas horizontally on the right.
- Relationship: Three equal lightweight benefits.
- Best for: Icon-style benefits, short labels, product states, interface fragments.

### 模块-3个利益点-左右布局.png

- Structure: Title area; three equal content areas side by side.
- Relationship: Three benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Three parallel features, selling-point cards, or benefit entries.

## 4 Benefit Points

### 模块-4个利益点-上下布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; one large content area; four small content areas below.
- Relationship: One core benefit plus four auxiliary benefits.
- Best for: A main value statement supported by four proof points.

### 模块-4个利益点-上下布局.png

- Structure: Title area; four equal content areas stacked vertically.
- Relationship: Four benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Four explanations, four steps, or four benefit groups.

### 模块-4个利益点-包含布局.png

- Structure: Title area; one large container with three smaller areas embedded on the right.
- Relationship: Four benefits form one integrated solution or product system.
- Best for: Packaging four benefits into a cohesive visual scene.

### 模块-4个利益点-四宫格布局.png

- Structure: Title area; 2x2 grid.
- Relationship: Four benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Four parallel selling points, services, or functional entries.

### 模块-4个利益点-左右布局.png

- Structure: Title area; four content areas in one horizontal row.
- Relationship: Four benefits are equally important.
- Best for: Desktop modules with very short benefit text.

## 5 Benefit Points

### 模块-5个利益点-上下布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; one large content area; five small content areas below.
- Relationship: One core benefit plus five auxiliary benefits.
- Best for: One total selling point with five concrete rights, functions, or proof points.

### 模块-5个利益点-包含布局.png

- Structure: Title area; one large container; four smaller areas arranged as 2x2 on the right.
- Relationship: Five benefits inside one integrated package, often a core area plus four supporting areas.
- Best for: Product matrices, benefit packages, campaign combinations, complex feature collections.

### 模块-5个利益点-左右布局-主次.png

- Structure: Title area; large left content area; 2x2 grid on the right.
- Relationship: One core benefit plus four auxiliary benefits.
- Best for: Desktop display with one main selling point and four support points.
